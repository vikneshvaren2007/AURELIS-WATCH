7-Day Return & Refund Policy

1. 7-Day Inspection Window

Collectors have a full 7 calendar days from the date of physical receipt to inspect their timepiece and initiate a return or exchange. This period is strictly honored across all product variants.

2. Return Eligibility Criteria

The timepiece must be returned in pristine, unworn condition with all protective plastic films intact, enclosed within its original presentation box, certificate of authenticity, and warranty booklet.

3. Refund Processing

For prepaid UPI / Razorpay orders, refunds are credited back to the original funding account within 3 to 5 business days after inspection. For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are dispatched via NEFT/IMPS bank transfer or UPI transfer as coordinated by our concierge desk.

4. Concierge Assistance

For urgent requests, contact our Master Horologist directly at +91 9445437069 or visit our atelier at Tenkasi, Main Road, opposite the bus stand.
