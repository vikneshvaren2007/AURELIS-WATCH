METICULOUS DISCIPLINE

The Four Pillars of AURELIS

From the raw billet of forged metallurgical steel to the final hand-regulation under stereoscopic magnification, no detail is left to compromise.

01

316L Surgical Stainless Steel

Forged under 500 metric tons of hydraulic pressure, our monobloc watch cases are milled from solid billets of 316L low-carbon austenitic steel. Features dual-surface finishing: hand-grained satin brush on vertical flanks contrasting with mirror-polished angled chamfers.

02

Flame-Fusion Sapphire Crystal

Rated at Mohs 9 hardness — second only to natural diamond — our watch crystals are synthesized through Verneuil flame-fusion crystallisation. Coated with multi-layered internal and external anti-reflective coatings for optical clarity under any lighting angle.

03

High Horology Caliber Regulation

Every mechanical balance wheel beats at 28,800 vibrations per hour (4Hz) or ultra-precise chronometric quartz synchronization. Hand-adjusted across five anatomical wrist positions and three temperature variances before leaving the atelier bench.

04

Atelier Assembly & 7-Stage QC

Constructed within an ISO Class 7 cleanroom environment to eliminate particulate contamination. Each finished piece endures 5 ATM / 50-meter hydrostatic pressure testing, 48-hour chronometer timing runs, and microscopic dial alignment inspection.
