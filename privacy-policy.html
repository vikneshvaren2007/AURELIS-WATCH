DATA GOVERNANCE

Privacy Policy

AURELIS respects the confidentiality and discretion of our horology collectors. This policy outlines how information gathered through our digital atelier is protected.

1. Data Collected: We collect personal details strictly necessary for order fulfillment, including full name, billing/shipping address, email address, and telephone number.

2. Payment Security: We do not store credit card credentials or UPI PIN numbers. Payment transactions are processed via RBI-authorized 256-bit encrypted gateways with server-side HMAC cryptographic signature verification.

3. Data Protection: User passwords are encrypted using one-way salted hashing. We never sell or distribute collector records to third-party marketing entities.
