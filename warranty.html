Certificate of Warranty Protection

AURELIS certifies that all 10 catalog timepieces are manufactured from 316L solid surgical stainless steel and high-precision horological calibers. For a period of six (6) months from the verified date of delivery, AURELIS guarantees the flawless operation of your timepiece's internal movement, crown assembly, and dial elements.

Term: 6 Months Active Coverage: Movement & Craft Concierge: Atelier Horology Desk Location: Tenkasi Atelier
✓

What is Protected (6 Months)

  • ✓ Caliber & Movement Timing: Quartz or mechanical timekeeping variances exceeding precision horological tolerances.
  • ✓ Chronograph Registers: Failure of split-second, 30-minute, or 24-hour subdial counters to actuate or reset.
  • ✓ Hands & Indices Attachment: Detachment or misalignment of hour, minute, second hands, or applied dial markers.
  • ✓ Crown & Stem Mechanism: Thread slippage, date quick-set malfunction, or inability to wind/adjust hands.
  • ✓ Case Flaws: Structural casting porosity or metallurgical defects present from manufacturing.
  • ✓ Complimentary Insured Pickup: Insured door-to-door reverse courier arranged across India.
✕

Warranty Exclusions

  • ✕ Normal Abrasive Wear: Superficial scratches on crystal, bezel, or metal bracelet from daily abrasive friction.
  • ✕ Crown-Open Water Intrusion: Water damage caused by failure to fully screw down or push the crown prior to immersion.
  • ✕ Extreme Impact Shock: Cracked sapphire crystal or dislodged internal balance wheel resulting from violent drops.
  • ✕ Unauthorized Modifications: Tampering, opening, or battery replacement performed by uncertified third-party jewellers.
  • ✕ Strap Degradation: Natural leather patina, water discoloration, or cosmetic strap wear beyond 30 days.
