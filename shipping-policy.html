Shipping & Delivery Policy

1. Complimentary Insured Air Transit

Every commission acquired through AURELIS receives complimentary door-to-door express courier shipping across India. All packages are fully insured against loss, theft, or damage in transit.

2. Transit Timeframe

Standard delivery duration is 3 to 5 business days following atelier inspection. Dispatches originate from our master atelier in Tenkasi, Tamil Nadu, under the personal supervision of our Master Horologist.

3. Tamper-Evident Security Packaging

Each timepiece is enclosed in a lacquered piano wood presentation box, sealed with an authentic holographic atelier tape. Customers must verify the integrity of the seal upon receipt.
