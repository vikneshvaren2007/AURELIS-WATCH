THE FOUNDING PHILOSOPHY

Where Mythological Artistry
Meets Architectural Steel.

AURELIS was born from a singular obsession: to forge timepieces that refuse to whisper. In an industry dominated by safe minimalism, our atelier looks to ancient imperial mythology and modern architectural engineering to create unapologetic sculptural statements.

The Dragon Imperium collection embodies this spirit. Taking inspiration from the Eastern dragon—a universal symbol of majesty, wisdom, and unstoppable strength—our watchmakers sculpted a three-dimensional dragon in high relief across three chronograph sub-registers.

Each case is milled from solid billets of surgical-grade 316L stainless steel, precision ion-plated in 18K yellow gold, and topped with our signature 14-sided polyhedral faceted bezel.
