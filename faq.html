Frequently Asked Questions

What are the two available watch colour variants?

We offer two masterwork variants: (1) Dragon Imperial — Two-Tone Surgical Steel and 18K Yellow Gold with White Dragon Guilloché Dial, and (2) Dragon Sovereign — Full 18K Yellow Gold Plated Case and Bracelet with Imperial Midnight Blue Dragon Dial. Both feature our signature 3D sculpted dragon.

What payment methods are supported?

We support two secure acquisition methods: (1) Google Pay / UPI via our cryptographically verified Razorpay payment gateway, supporting all UPI applications (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), and (2) Cash on Delivery (COD) with full inspection privilege.

How long does insured shipping take?

Commissions are packed in lacquered wooden presentation cases and dispatched via priority air express (BlueDart/FedEx). Transit across India typically takes 3 to 5 business days with real-time tracking.

What is the 7-day return and refund policy?

You have 7 days from the verified delivery date to request a return or exchange through our self-service portal or by contacting our Master Horologist (+91 9445437069). Prepaid orders are refunded directly to the original UPI/payment source once received.

What warranty is included with my timepiece?

Every AURELIS timepiece is protected by an official 6-Month Manufacturer Warranty covering caliber accuracy, movement parts, hands assembly, and crown mechanisms. Read our complete 6-Month Warranty Policy.
